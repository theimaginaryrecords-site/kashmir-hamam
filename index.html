Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Kashmiri hamam cost?
Every room is different, so we quote per project after a free site visit. As a rough idea, most home hamams fall in a range set by room size, stone quantity and firebox work. Share your room size on the quote form and we will call back with an honest estimate — no obligation.
How long does it take to build?
A standard room hamam usually takes 7 to 12 working days from site preparation to handover. Larger halls and mosque projects take longer. We give you a clear timeline before starting.
Which stone do you use?
Only original Devar stone — the dense natural stone Kashmiri hamams have been built from for generations. It absorbs heat slowly and releases it for 24 to 36 hours. We never substitute cheaper stone.
What maintenance does a hamam need?
Very little. Sweep the firebox ash regularly, reseal the stone joints once a year, and check the chimney before winter. Our maintenance kits cover everything, and we offer repair visits across the valley.
What does the lifetime guarantee cover?
Our craftsmanship and the stone floor structure — if the hamam floor, firebox brickwork or stonework we built ever fails in normal use, we repair it free. It covers our work, not damage from misuse or structural changes to the building.
